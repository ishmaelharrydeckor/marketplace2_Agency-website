Personal Brand Site

Samuel Adanuvo — Personal Brand & Speaking Website

A personal brand website for a business growth consultant, author and keynote speaker, built to bring in consulting clients and speaking invitations.

Visit the live siteStart your project
IndustryConsulting, speaking & education
Project typePersonal brand website
Main actionsBook a call, book a talk

Brief

Samuel Adanuvo is a consultant, author, speaker and event host. His website has to present all of that clearly, without leaving a visitor unsure where to start.

Two actions matter most: booking a free strategy call, and booking him to speak.

Scope of Work

  • Website design and development
  • Content structure for consulting, speaking, events and a book
  • Enquiry form with enquiry types
  • Video, testimonial and FAQ sections
  • Responsive build

Execution

A confident first screen

The headline, "The literal cheat code for business growth.", sits beside a one-line description of his roles and two buttons: "Book a free strategy call" and "Book Samuel to speak".

Proof early on

Three numbers appear near the top: 120+ young leaders at a summit, 2000+ young entrepreneurs reached, and 5+ years convening events.

Clear offers

Consulting is presented as "The Growth Engine", with six pillars: clarity, visibility, conversion, acceleration, loyalty and handover. Speaking has its own section with topics and booking.

Events, video and the book

Past and upcoming events, a set of video insights, client testimonials, and a waitlist for his book keep the site active and give visitors reasons to return.

Easy enquiries

The contact form lets visitors choose why they are writing (consulting, speaking, events or other), and the page states how quickly the team replies.

Outcomes

What visitors can do

  • Book a free strategy call
  • Book Samuel to speak
  • See past events and what is coming next
  • Watch video insights and read client testimonials
  • Join the waitlist for his book

What This Project Shows

Presenting one person with several roles as a single, clear brand, and sending each type of visitor to the right action.
