Product Website

ChecIN — Attendance Platform Website

A product website for ChecIN, an attendance tool where a QR code on an entrance tablet changes every 15 seconds and employees scan it with their phones.

Visit the live siteStart your project
IndustryWorkforce & HR technology
Project typeProduct website
StatusPilot teams being onboarded

Brief

Most attendance tools trust the employee's phone. ChecIN trusts the door. That difference is the whole product, so the site has to make it clear very quickly.

It also has to speak to everyone who touches attendance, from employees to finance, and move interested teams toward a free trial.

Scope of Work

  • Product website design and development
  • Product messaging and feature explanation
  • Pricing section with three plans
  • FAQ and calls to action
  • Responsive build

Execution

Making the idea obvious

The headline, "Attendance that proves people were actually there.", leads straight into how it works: a code on an entrance tablet changes every 15 seconds, so there is no GPS to spoof, no screenshot to pass around and no timesheets to chase.

Features and benefits

The page explains the product in plain benefits, such as "Less chasing. More clarity.", and lists four core capabilities:

  • Real-time attendance with a live team view
  • Leave and absence management
  • Announcements and team updates
  • Tamper-resistant records

One platform for everyone

A section speaks to the roles behind attendance: owners and HR, team managers, employees, operations, finance and leadership.

Pricing and questions

Three plans are laid out clearly: a free Starter plan for small teams, a per-user Growth plan with a monthly and yearly toggle, and a custom Enterprise plan. A FAQ answers the questions buyers ask first, such as whether a screenshot can be used to check in for someone else.

Outcomes

What visitors can do

  • Start a free trial or open the live kiosk
  • Compare the three plans
  • Get answers on hardware, apps and data separation
  • Ask to join the launch program

What This Project Shows

Explaining a product with a clear single idea, and guiding different kinds of buyers to the right next step.
