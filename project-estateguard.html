EstateGuard — Government Housing & Rent Payment Platform

A landing page for EstateGuard, a platform where verified government employees find approved homes and pay rent by approving a prompt on their phone.

Visit the live siteStart your project
IndustryHousing & rent payments
Project typePlatform landing page
AudienceVerified government employees

Brief

EstateGuard offers a new way to rent a home, and it is aimed at one specific audience: verified government employees.

The site has to explain the offer in seconds, show how rent is paid, and build trust in a service that involves both housing and money.

Scope of Work

  • Landing page design and development
  • Messaging and page structure
  • Calls to action for visitors at different stages
  • Responsive build for phones and desktops

Execution

One-line message

The headline, "Earn your salary. Pay your home directly.", states the offer at once. A sentence below says who it is for and how it works: find approved homes and pay rent by approving a prompt on your phone.

Trust signals

The page puts its safeguards where visitors can see them:

  • Identity-verified tenants and landlords
  • Inspected properties
  • Digital tenancy agreements
  • Mobile-money or bank approval
  • Digital receipts and daily payment checks
  • A tamper-evident audit trail

Two ways in

Two calls to action match two levels of readiness: "Request a briefing" for visitors who want to learn more, and "Get started" for those ready to begin.

Outcomes

What visitors can do

  • Understand the offer from the headline alone
  • See how rent is paid and what protects both sides
  • Request a briefing or get started straight away

What This Project Shows

Taking a service that involves trust, money and housing and turning it into a page that explains itself in seconds.
