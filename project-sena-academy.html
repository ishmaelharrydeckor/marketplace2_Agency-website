Sena Academy — AI Design Course Platform

A platform for selling and delivering a course on creating websites with AI design tools, currently in development.

Visit the live siteStart your project
IndustryOnline education
Project typeCourse and learning platform
StatusIn development

Brief

Sena Academy teaches people to design websites using AI tools, aimed at founders, designers and marketers.

The site needs to explain what the course covers, show the templates that come with it, and let people choose a plan.

Scope of Work

  • Course platform website
  • Curriculum and templates presentation
  • Pricing plans
  • FAQ and calls to action

Execution

Selling the skill

The page leads with a clear promise: learn to build AI-designed websites in a few days, with no coding required.

Showing what is inside

A curriculum lists the lessons from first AI website to full projects, alongside page blueprints, launch checklists and a set of ready-made templates.

Choosing a plan

Two plans are shown side by side, one for the course and one that adds unlimited templates and motion videos.

Outcomes

What visitors will be able to do

  • See what the course covers
  • Browse the included templates
  • Choose a plan and get started

What This Project Shows

Presenting an education product so that visitors can see what they would learn and what they would get.
