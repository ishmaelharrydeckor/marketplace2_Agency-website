Solara Residence — Luxury Apartment Development Website

A website for a luxury apartment development in East Legon Hills, Accra, covering available homes, floors, amenities, the investment case and build progress.

Visit the live siteStart your project
IndustryReal estate development
Project typeProperty development website
StatusConcept build

Brief

A property development has to do several jobs at once: show what is for sale, make a case for buying, and prove the building is really going up.

The site was built to let a buyer or investor explore all of that in one place and end with an enquiry.

Scope of Work

  • Website design and development
  • Interactive floor finder
  • Investment simulator
  • Construction progress tracker
  • Developer comparison and enquiry form
  • Responsive build

Execution

Finding a home

A unit inventory shows each apartment type with its price, how many are left and a booking button. An interactive floor finder lets visitors select a floor on the building facade to see what is there.

Making the case

An investment section explains why the area is attractive and includes a simulator: choose a unit type and a timeframe, and see an estimated value and return.

Building trust

A construction tracker shows overall completion and each stage from groundbreaking to finishing. A comparison table sets the project beside two other developments from the same developer.

Turning interest into an enquiry

A registration form captures the details and the unit of interest, and a booking preview explains how a deposit locks a unit.

Outcomes

What visitors can do

  • Compare unit types and prices
  • Explore the building floor by floor
  • Estimate a return on investment
  • Follow construction progress
  • Register interest in a unit

What This Project Shows

Turning a large, detailed property offer into something a buyer can explore step by step, with proof at every stage.
